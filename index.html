HOW TO PLAY +

Nine-ball. Always strike the lowest-numbered ball first. Pocket any ball to keep shooting. Sink the 9-ball on a legal shot to win.

Controls. Move your pointer to aim. Hold the pointer or Space to charge, release to strike. Arrow keys fine-aim. Esc pauses the room.

Fouls. Wrong ball first, a scratch, or no rail after contact hands your opponent ball in hand. Three fouls in a row loses the rack.
